Build
Number of build configurations (distinct sets of hardware, firmware, or requirements) managed in the system.
Boards
Physical units produced or tracked as part of a build request or stored in the warehouse.
Build Plan
A structured plan outlining the requirements, timeline, and specifications for a new product introduction (NPI) build.
Build Request
A formal request to produce a specific quantity of boards under a given build plan and revision.
Family
Product family or group, used to categorize builds and analytics.
Form Factor
The physical and technical specification category for a board or product.
Milestone Build
A build plan labeled as a key milestone or support activity in the NPI process.
Support Activity
Special activities or labels (e.g., Milestone) associated with builds for tracking and reporting.
PCB Revision
Version of the printed circuit board design used in a build.
HW Revision
Hardware revision identifier for a build or board.
Silicon Stepping
The version or stepping of the silicon used in a build.
Supplier
Vendor or partner providing components or boards for a build.
ODM
Original Design Manufacturer, a partner involved in the NPI process.
PM
Program Manager, responsible for managing build plans and requests.
